Privacy Policy for Prism Sort

Application Name: Prism Sort (Package ID: com.aistudio.prismsort.kxqmzp)

Privacy Summary: Prism Sort is an offline-capable puzzle game. We do not collect, transmit, rent, or sell any personal data, personally identifiable information (PII), location data, contacts, or device identifiers to any external servers or third parties.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Prism Sort ("we", "our", or "the Application") handles information when you install and play our mobile application distributed via Google Play. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with Google Play Developer Program Policies and applicable data protection laws (including GDPR and CCPA).

2. Information Collection and Use

Prism Sort does not collect or transmit any personal or sensitive user data. Specifically:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, phone number, or payment details to play.
  • No Analytics or Tracking SDKs: The Application does not embed third-party advertising networks, behavioral tracking SDKs, or cross-app profiling tools.
  • No Location or Media Access: The Application never requests access to your GPS location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or files.

3. Local On-Device Data Storage

To provide seamless gameplay, the Application stores your game state strictly locally on your device using Android's standard internal application storage (SQLite / Jetpack Room database):

  • Current campaign level and completed level history (Levels 1–5,000).
  • In-progress glass tube and ball arrangement so you can resume your puzzle.
  • Earned in-game Star Coins balance and unlocked cosmetic themes (Ball Skins, Tube Themes, and Backgrounds).
  • User preferences for Sound Effects and Haptic Vibration toggles.

This gameplay data resides solely inside your device's sandboxed app directory (and your personal Android OS device backup if enabled in your system settings) and is never transmitted to us.

4. Device Permissions Used

Prism Sort requests only the following minimal, non-sensitive Android system permission:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE — Used exclusively to provide optional tactile haptic feedback when selecting spheres, completing color stacks, or completing levels. You can disable haptics at any time inside the in-game Settings menu.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all game progress and settings are stored locally on your device, you have full control over your data at all times. You may permanently delete all stored game data by:

  • Opening your Android device's Settings > Apps > Prism Sort > Storage and tapping Clear Storage / Clear Data; or
  • Uninstalling the Prism Sort application from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Prism Sort does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). Because the Application collects no personal data from any user, it is safe for general audiences.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if new features are introduced. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
